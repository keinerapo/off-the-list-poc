Local processing: downloaded official Commons 1280px thumbnails with curl,
then resized to 1000px width with macOS sips. No crop or color edits.
For the original three photographs, direct 1000px thumbnail URLs returned
HTTP 400. For the new three, the Commons imageinfo API requested at 1000px
returned official 1280px thumbnail URLs. attribution.json records the actual
successful download URLs. New files were resized with sips -Z 1000 to a
maximum dimension of 1000px; no crop or color edits.
